An address book that cannot phone home.

Seca Contacts has no Internet permission at all, so your contacts cannot leave this phone through it. They stay in Android’s own address book, where DAVx⁵ or any sync of your choice can still reach them.

One of three apps — Contacts, Phone and Messages — sharing one Material 3 Expressive design. Android 12 or newer, with or without Google, at its best on GrapheneOS. In English, French, German, Spanish and Polish.

<b>Your contacts stay on your phone</b>
<ul>
<li>No Internet permission: the app cannot send anything anywhere.</li>
<li>Contacts stay in Android's own address book, where DAVx⁵ or any sync of your choice can reach them.</li>
<li>Works with GrapheneOS Contact Scopes.</li>
</ul>

<b>Organised the way you live</b>
<ul>
<li>Coloured profiles (Family, Work…), whose colour carries over to Seca Phone and Seca Messages.</li>
<li>Favourites, instant search, fast scrolling by letter.</li>
<li>Duplicates found by name or number, merged without losing anything.</li>
<li>Your own card, shared as a QR code.</li>
</ul>

<b>Also</b>
<ul>
<li>vCard import and export.</li>
<li>Password-encrypted backup.</li>
<li>App lock, and a hidden preview in recent apps.</li>
<li>Wallpaper colours or Seca palettes, dark theme.</li>
</ul>

No ads, no trackers, no account. Open source.
