An address is kept as its parts — street, postcode, town, region, country — so it leaves this phone whole instead of crammed into one line, and can be edited a field at a time. A photograph can be framed before it becomes somebody’s face: drag and pinch until the face is in the circle. A changed or removed photograph now shows at once instead of after a restart. A birthday says how old that makes them. A favourite shows its whole name, and the row shows that it scrolls. The editor was rebuilt to look like the page that shows a contact. Seca Contacts can now answer when another app asks for one contact, and can open a contact card it is sent. In German, thanks to Natty-Narwhal.
