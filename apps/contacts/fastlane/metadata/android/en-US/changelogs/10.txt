A contact can be given a photo at last, and the editor shows the one they already have instead of drawing initials over it. Photos now travel in a backup too: the vCard had no line for them, so every export and every restore quietly lost them. A number, an address or an e-mail can be called what you call it — "Holiday house" — and that name survives a backup as well. Anniversaries are read and written beside birthdays. A new contact says where it is about to be saved, and lets you choose when the phone has more than one place for it. Backups can write themselves now: a folder you pick, every day or every week, with an optional second copy in formats anything can read.
