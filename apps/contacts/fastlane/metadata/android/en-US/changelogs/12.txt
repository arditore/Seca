Holding a number, an e-mail or an address copies it, on the contact's page and in the list.

The default number and e-mail can be chosen while editing a contact. Android has had a flag for this all along and Seca never read it, so a contact with a work number and a mobile was called on whichever the provider happened to return first. The chosen one now comes first everywhere a contact is shown by a single number.

A birthday or an anniversary opens the calendar on the day it next falls.

Favourites can be arranged by hand. Alphabetical stays the default.

The shape faces are cut to is yours to choose: the playful one per person as before, or one shape for everybody, or one for a single contact from their own card.

A palette of your own, mixed from a hue and an amount of colour.

The launcher can say "Contacts" rather than "Seca Contacts". One switch renames all three apps.

The settings are a page of pages now rather than one long scroll.

German throughout, including the parts shared with the other two apps.
