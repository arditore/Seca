Picture messages arrive. Seca is the only app the phone hands them to, and until now it did nothing with them: the picture never came, and nobody was told. They are fetched, shown in the conversation beside the texts, with the words sent alongside them, and a conversation made only of pictures appears in the list at last. Sending one is still to come. Delivery reports can be turned on, so a text says Delivered rather than Sent where the carrier answers. Notifications from a service no longer pile into one. India's TRAI sender rules are followed. With two SIMs, holding Send down sends that one message from the other. Backups can write themselves now: a folder you pick, every day or every week.
