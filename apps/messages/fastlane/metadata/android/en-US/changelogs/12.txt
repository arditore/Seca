Picture messages reach the other telephone. The recipient's address was written the way the sender's is, so the carrier took every message and had nowhere to send it, while this phone showed it as sent. None could arrive until now. The app no longer dies on a message it cannot read. Pinch to zoom a received photo and save it from there; GIFs move; four pictures per message. The archive and the advertising can be emptied again. A sound when a message leaves, and one for arriving.
