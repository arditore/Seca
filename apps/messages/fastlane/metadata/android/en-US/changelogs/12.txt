Picture messages reach the other telephone. The recipient was written the way the sender is written: To, Cc and Bcc each hold one plain encoded string, while From is the one address field with a length and a token in front of it. Every message went out with that token where the carrier expected the character set, so the address came out unreadable, the server accepted the message and had nowhere to send it — and the sending phone showed it as sent, because as far as it knew it had been. The spaces a number is written with went into the address too. No picture message could arrive anywhere until now.

The app no longer dies on a picture message it cannot read. The three receivers that handle multimedia parsed bytes from the carrier and wrote to the message store on the thread that draws, with nothing to catch a failure: a malformed message took the whole app down, and a large photo arriving froze it.

A received photo can be looked at properly: pinch to zoom, drag to move about, double tap to go in and out. Saving is on that screen, where you are when you decide to keep it.

GIFs move, in the conversation and opened. A GIF decoded into a bitmap is its first frame and nothing else, which is how every one of them arrived — and one that fits is now sent as it is rather than flattened.

Up to four pictures in one message.

The archive and the advertising can be emptied again. Nothing put in them could come back out: holding a row there did nothing, no bar ever appeared, and the conversation offers no way out either, so archiving lost a conversation as surely as deleting it.

A sound when a message leaves, off by default, at the volume set for notifications and never when the phone is silent. And a sound of your own for arriving messages, chosen on Android's own page for them.

Holding a conversation chooses it, and says so whether or not conversations are chosen already. Everything one conversation can be put through is on the bar above once it is chosen, or inside the conversation.

The shape faces are cut to, and the palette, follow what Seca Contacts is set to, including a palette you mixed yourself.

The launcher can say "Messages" rather than "Seca Messages". One switch renames all three apps.

The settings are a page of pages now rather than one long scroll.

German throughout, including Seca Link and the parts shared with the other two apps.
