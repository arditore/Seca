A group conversation is the group, not whoever wrote last. What you attach waits in the composer until you send it. A long text in a language with accents goes in several parts, and every part now reports back: one that arrived no longer shows as failed. Delivery reports work at all, for the first time. The carrier says how large a message may be, not us. A message is announced unless you are reading that very conversation. The archive sits at the top.
