Picture messages can be sent now, not only received. The plus beside the text offers a picture, a video, a file or a contact card, sent through your carrier so it reaches any phone. Hold the microphone to record a voice message; it travels by carrier too where there is no Seca Link. A recording can be saved and played faster, and one sent by another app is recognised as a recording rather than shown as a file. Links in a message can be touched. Several conversations can be chosen at once. The name at the top opens the person. Advertising that offers its way out as a link is filed too. Messages are built the way the standard asks, so accents and pictures arrive correctly on phones that are not Seca. In German, thanks to Natty-Narwhal.
