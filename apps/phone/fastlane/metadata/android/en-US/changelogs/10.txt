A second call can be started without leaving the call screen: "Add call" opens a keypad, and Merge and Swap appear once both are up. The history's filters can be swiped between instead of reached for at the top of the screen. The four sentences offered when you decline a call with a message can be your own, in Settings. With two SIMs, holding the call button down uses the other one for that call only, without changing what the contact is called with afterwards. Backups can write themselves now: a folder you pick, every day or every week.
